### "7 AI Terms Every Class 5–10 Student Should Know"

**Slide 1 / Hook**

**Your child uses AI every single day.**

---

**Slide 2 / Term #1**

**🤖 Algorithm**

A set of instructions that tells a computer what to do — step by step.

*Google uses an algorithm to rank search results. Netflix uses one to recommend shows.*

*Student version: "The recipe AI follows to make decisions."*

---

**Slide 3 / Term #2**

**🧠 Neural Network**

A system that loosely mimics the human brain — layers of connected nodes that process information.

*Used in face recognition, voice assistants, self-driving cars.*

*Student version: "AI's brain — made of connected layers that learn from examples."*

---

**Slide 4 / Term #3**

**📊 Training Data**

The examples used to teach AI how to recognize patterns.

*Show AI 10,000 cat photos — that's training data. The more, the smarter.*

*Student version: "The textbook AI studies from."*

---

**Slide 5 / Term #4**

**🔍 Bias in AI**

When AI reflects unfair patterns from its training data.

*Example: An AI trained mostly on male doctors' data might perform worse for female patients.*

*Student version: "When AI learns the wrong lessons from bad examples."*

---

**Slide 6 / Term #5 + #6**

**🗣️ Large Language Model (LLM)**

AI trained on massive amounts of text to understand and generate language.

*ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude — all LLMs.*

*Student version: "An AI that read the entire internet and learned to speak."*

**🔁 Generative AI**

AI that creates new content — text, images, video, code — based on patterns it learned.

*Student version: "AI that makes new things, not just finds old ones."*

---

**Slide 7 / Term #7 + Recap**

**⚡ Inference**

When AI uses what it learned to respond to new inputs — the "thinking" phase.

*Student version: "When AI actually uses its brain to answer your question."*

**Quick recap:**

1. Algorithm — AI's recipe
2. Neural Network — AI's brain
3. Training Data — AI's textbook
4. Bias — AI's wrong lessons
5. LLM — AI that read the internet
6. Generative AI — AI that creates
7. Inference — AI thinking live
